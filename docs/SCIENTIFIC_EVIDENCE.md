# Scientific evidence registry

## Published astronaut research

| Source | Traceable contribution to LIMEN | Boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Clément et al. (2022), *Frontiers in Physiology*, DOI [10.3389/fphys.2022.1029161](https://doi.org/10.3389/fphys.2022.1029161) | Context on human adaptation to the spaceflight environment and sensorimotor/vestibular considerations. | LIMEN does not reproduce the source dataset. |
| Tays et al. (2025), *Cerebral Cortex*, DOI [10.1093/cercor/bhae502](https://doi.org/10.1093/cercor/bhae502) | A 45° clockwise visual-feedback rotation and measures including direction error, movement time, and reaction time motivate the planned task. The study reported behavioural and neural correlates across astronaut and control cohorts. | Browser mouse/trackpad input is not the study's joystick implementation; LIMEN does not measure neural activity. |
| Hupfeld et al. (2022), *Brain and behavioral evidence for reweighting of vestibular inputs with long-duration spaceflight* | Supports the scientific context that vestibular inputs, balance, mobility, and spatial orientation adapt with long-duration spaceflight. | No vestibular stimulation or fMRI is performed by LIMEN. |
| NASA Human Performance Standard, §4.2 Fitness-for-Duty Sensorimotor | Provides operational context for task-specific sensorimotor assessment and post-mission reconditioning. | It does not validate this prototype or set LIMEN thresholds. |
| NASA CIPHER and Vestibular Health research | Provides programme context for longitudinal astronaut health and vestibular research. | LIMEN has no access to CIPHER or astronaut-level raw data. |

## Evidence-use policy

Published findings remain cited findings. Browser task outputs are user-generated prototype measurements. Any interface examples must be labeled **simulated demonstration data** and cannot be presented as astronaut data or observed research outcomes.
