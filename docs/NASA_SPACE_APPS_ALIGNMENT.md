# NASA Space Apps alignment

LIMEN is positioned as a NASA Space Apps 2026 scientific software prototype. It translates publicly described research on sensorimotor and vestibular adaptation into a transparent, browser-based concept for repeated task observation.

The alignment is conceptual:

- Human spaceflight introduces gravity-transition demands on sensorimotor and vestibular function.
- Longitudinal, task-specific assessment is relevant to understanding adaptation and reconditioning contexts.
- Visual, evidence-linked summaries can help communicate uncertainty and individual baseline comparisons.

LIMEN does not claim NASA endorsement, NASA operational deployment, access to NASA internal systems, or compliance certification. NASA standards and research programmes are cited as context rather than product requirements fulfilled by this Phase 1 shell.
