# LIMEN research charter

## Purpose

LIMEN is a research and operational decision-support prototype for understanding sensorimotor adaptation and readaptation around gravity transitions in human spaceflight. It focuses on task-level behaviour in a browser-based visuomotor task inspired by published rotation paradigms.

## Research question

How does human sensorimotor performance change during altered-gravity adaptation and subsequent readaptation, and how can repeated task measurements be compared with an individual's own baseline?

## Scope

Future phases may record mouse or trackpad movement during a shortened, configurable browser task with normal, rotated-feedback, and de-adaptation phases. This differs from laboratory joystick protocols and is not a reproduction or clinical validation of any published protocol.

LIMEN is not a general health dashboard, EEG platform, neuroimaging viewer, medical device, or NASA-endorsed product. It will not infer neural activity from movement data.

## Decision boundaries

Interpretation remains descriptive and baseline-relative. No single score represents medical readiness, no recovery threshold is invented, and incomplete or missing trials are not substituted with zero.
